## Timeless Question
When does sparing someone the weight of a true account begin to leave others carrying a false one?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Shielded Regard (the practiced refusal to let a person absorb the full public cost of a true but wounding account when that cost can still be borne privately or deferred without collapsing the common trust)
- Domain B: Shared Record (the recognition that an account kept incomplete or softened so one person is spared will later be used by others who have no choice but to act on the version they were given)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where mercy toward one and fidelity to the common account become two conditions of the same honesty — a sparing that stays kind because it has not made the record unusable for those who must live by it.

**Color of the Overlap:** River Stone  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Measured Mercy — the practiced capacity to lighten a person's load without pretending the shared account can be rewritten for their comfort alone.
