Mosaic Daily Tile — 2026-09-30

Timeless Question

When does the duty to keep a faithful record begin to freeze the living person that the record was meant to serve?

Venn Domains

  • Domain A: Faithful Record (the disciplined work of keeping what happened available so that learning, repair, and justice can still find their facts)
  • Domain B: Merciful Forgetting (the recognition that some traces, once held too tightly, stop a person or a community from becoming anything other than their worst documented hour)

Overlap: The living hinge where remembering enough to remain honest and releasing enough to remain free become two conditions of the same care — a memory that still leaves room for a next chapter.

Color of the Overlap: Pale Slate
Mindset of the Overlap: Stewarded Memory — the practiced capacity to keep what must not be denied while refusing to let the archive become a cage.

Anonymous Ballot

Question: When a past act is still on the record and a later life has begun to grow past it, which orientation do you trust first?

  • A) Keep the record fully visible; forgetting is more often convenience than mercy
  • B) Allow the older mark to recede; otherwise the person is only ever their worst documented hour
  • C) Keep a private or limited record that can be reopened if harm returns, while letting public life move on
  • D) Other / it depends (explain below)

Optional reflection (anonymous long field):
What recent record — a file, a story retold, a score kept — felt both necessary for honesty and costly to the person who still has to live after it?
