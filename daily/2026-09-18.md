## Timeless Question
When does the search for certainty begin to close the very questions that keep understanding alive?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Epistemic Rigor (the disciplined demand for evidence, coherence, and the refusal of comfortable illusions)
- Domain B: Open Inquiry (the willingness to dwell in ambiguity, revise premises, and follow evidence wherever it leads—even into discomfort)

**Overlap:** The narrow band where the hunger for solid ground meets the necessity of remaining permeable to what might overturn that ground.

**Color of the Overlap:** Warm Ochre  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Intellectual Humility — the practiced capacity to hold a conviction firmly while still treating it as provisional, and to treat the unknown not as a threat but as the condition of continued thought.
