## Timeless Question
When does the demand for transparent accountability begin to hollow out the private spaces in which trust, apology, and second chances can still take root?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Transparent Accountability (the insistence that actions be visible, measurable, and answerable to a shared standard so that power cannot hide)
- Domain B: Protective Privacy (the recognition that certain human movements—regret, repair, quiet growth—require shelter from constant observation if they are to remain genuine)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where visibility and shelter become two conditions of the same moral regard — a light that illuminates without scorching the ground where people still need to become better than their last visible act.

**Color of the Overlap:** Pale Indigo  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Sheltered Answerability — the practiced capacity to keep the door open for accountability while still guarding the rooms in which people may revise themselves without performing the revision for an audience.
