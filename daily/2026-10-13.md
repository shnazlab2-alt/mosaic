## Timeless Question
When does forgiving a wrong begin to require the memory of it to disappear before the release can be trusted?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Released Account (the practiced refusal to keep extracting further payment from a person once the debt of acknowledgment or restitution has already been named and accepted)
- Domain B: Kept Memory (the recognition that a wrong whose claim has been lifted can still remain as the map by which the forgiver, and anyone who was not present, continues to read what actually occurred)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where release and remembrance become two conditions of the same justice — a forgiveness that stays true because it has not demanded the event vanish in order for the claim to end.

**Color of the Overlap:** Pale Copper  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Remembered Release — the practiced capacity to let a claim go without pretending the mark that generated it has left no residue.
