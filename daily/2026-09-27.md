# Mosaic Daily Tile — 2026-09-27

## Timeless Question
When does the pursuit of seamless efficiency begin to dissolve the generative friction that alone teaches us what is worth keeping slow?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Frictionless Efficiency (the disciplined removal of delay, redundancy, and resistance so that energy, attention, and resources flow without waste toward a clear end)
- Domain B: Generative Friction (the recognition that certain resistances, pauses, and difficulties are not defects but the very conditions under which judgment, attachment, skill, and meaning take root)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where clearing the path and preserving the necessary resistance become two conditions of the same care — a speed that still knows which obstacles are teachers rather than obstacles.

**Color of the Overlap:** Burnished Bronze  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Discerning Velocity — the practiced capacity to accelerate where friction is only waste, while deliberately keeping or even introducing the slower resistances that form character, care, and lasting competence.

- B) Protect or restore the friction; without it, speed becomes hollow and the deeper goods wither
