## Timeless Question
When does loyalty to the tested ways of the past begin to starve the living judgment that alone can keep those ways from becoming relics?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Received Tradition (the practices, judgments, and restraints that have already been tried against reality and found durable)
- Domain B: Fresh Discernment (the capacity to see and respond to conditions the tradition never encountered, even when that requires revision)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where honoring the tested and risking the new become two conditions of the same fidelity — a continuity that does not fossilize.

**Color of the Overlap:** Soft Amber  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Rooted Invention — the practiced capacity to let the inheritance speak first while still reserving the right and duty to revise it when the ground itself has shifted.
