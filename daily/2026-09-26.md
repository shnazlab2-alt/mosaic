## Timeless Question
When does the expectation of constant availability begin to erode the deeper goods that only intermittent presence can protect?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Immediate Presence (the demand that care, attention, and response happen in real time, face to face or near-instant, without delay or dilution)
- Domain B: Durable Absence (the recognition that rest, reflection, long projects, and genuine recovery require deliberate withdrawal from constant reachability)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where being answerable to others and remaining answerable to one’s own deeper work become two conditions of the same fidelity — a presence that does not consume the silence it needs in order to remain real.

**Color of the Overlap:** Soft Sage  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Rhythmic Availability — the practiced capacity to keep the door open without living permanently in the doorway, and to treat both arrival and departure as forms of care rather than betrayal.
