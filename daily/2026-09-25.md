## Timeless Question
When does handing a task to a capable mind begin to starve the human practices that made the task worth doing in the first place?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Human Craft (the slow, fallible, first-person work of making, judging, repairing, and meaning something with one's own hands and attention)
- Domain B: Machine Competence (the reliable, scalable, often faster execution of the same surface task by a system that does not get tired in our way)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where asking for help and remaining inside the work become two conditions of the same respect — competence that does not evict the person who still has to live with what was done.

**Color of the Overlap:** Weathered Copper  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Kept Hands — the practiced capacity to accept a capable partner without abandoning the room, the judgment, or the after-care that only a human neighbor can still owe.
