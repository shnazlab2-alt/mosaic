## Timeless Question
When does naming who made a thing begin to hide the hands that made the naming possible?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Named Credit (the practiced refusal to let a finished work float free of the person who carried it, so that praise, blame, and learning can still find an author)
- Domain B: Unnamed Making (the recognition that a finished thing often stands on hands, corrections, and prior work that a single name cannot hold without shrinking them)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where attribution and shared making become two conditions of the same honesty — a credit that stays true because it has not pretended the named person worked alone.

**Color of the Overlap:** Copper Linen  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Answerable Authorship — the practiced capacity to name a maker without erasing the hands the name depends on.
