## Timeless Question
When does loyalty to the story of who we have been begin to close the door on who we might still become?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Narrative Continuity (the coherent self we protect through memory, identity, and the refusal to abandon the past that formed us)
- Domain B: Adaptive Becoming (the capacity to revise, shed, or rewrite the self in response to new evidence, relationships, or conditions)

**Overlap:** The living edge where fidelity to one’s history meets the necessity of remaining unfinished.

**Color of the Overlap:** Soft Indigo  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Narrative Stewardship — the practiced willingness to honor the chapters already written while treating the next page as still blank, and to ask whether the story is serving the life or the life is serving the story.
