## Timeless Question
When does resting begin to abandon the work that rest was meant to make possible again?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Protected Rest (the practiced refusal to treat every hour as available to the next demand, so that attention, body, and judgment can return instead of only continuing)
- Domain B: Unfinished Charge (the recognition that some work does not pause when the worker does, and that rest taken past a real limit can shift the cost onto someone who did not choose the pause)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where stopping and remaining answerable become two conditions of the same stewardship — a rest that restores the worker without pretending the work has stopped.

**Color of the Overlap:** Hearth Ash  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Recovered Charge — the practiced capacity to stop without abandoning the charge, and to return before the stop becomes someone else's unpaid shift.
