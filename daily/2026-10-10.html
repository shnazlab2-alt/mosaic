Mosaic Daily Tile — 2026-10-10

Timeless Question

When does giving undivided presence to one person begin to leave the others who also needed it unattended?

Venn Domains

  • Domain A: Singular Presence (the practiced refusal to split attention so that the person in front of you receives a mind that is not already half elsewhere)
  • Domain B: Distributed Regard (the recognition that presence concentrated on one can leave others waiting in ways that later apology cannot restore)

Overlap: The living hinge where attending fully and remaining available become two conditions of the same care — a presence that stays true because it has not treated the rest of the circle as paused.

Color of the Overlap: Soft Amber
Mindset of the Overlap: Bounded Presence — the practiced capacity to give undivided attention without treating the boundary of that attention as the end of responsibility.

  • A) Stay fully present; a presence that keeps one eye on the next claim is more often performance than care
  • B) Widen the regard; attention that will not notice the waiting has already stopped being presence and become absorption
  • C) Name the limit of the present moment, hand off what cannot wait, and return the remainder of the attention
  • D) Other / it depends (explain below)
