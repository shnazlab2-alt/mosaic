## Timeless Question
When does the care not to judge too soon begin to leave standing the harm that judgment was meant to limit?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Measured Judgment (the disciplined refusal to name a fault, assign a consequence, or close a case until proportion, evidence, and the other side have had a real chance to appear)
- Domain B: Timely Intervention (the recognition that some harms thicken while the case is still being refined, and that waiting can itself become a decision about who bears the cost)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where holding the verdict and interrupting the harm become two conditions of the same fairness — a judgment that arrives early enough to matter and late enough to remain answerable.

**Color of the Overlap:** Iron Amber  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Proportionate Courage — the practiced capacity to act before certainty is complete without treating incomplete certainty as a license.
