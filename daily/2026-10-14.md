## Timeless Question
When does the work of making something durable begin to cost the people who must still live inside the unfinished version of it?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Durable Making (the practiced refusal to treat a thing as finished while the joints that will hold under later pressure are still loose)
- Domain B: Present Habitation (the recognition that the people who already inhabit the work cannot wait for durability before they need shelter, clarity, or ground that does not shift under them)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where finishing and dwelling become two conditions of the same craft — a making that stays responsible because it has not treated the unfinished as if it cost the inhabitants nothing.

**Color of the Overlap:** Weathered Oak
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Inhabited Craft — the practiced capacity to keep the joints true without pretending the people already living under the roof can be asked to wait outside until the last nail is driven.
