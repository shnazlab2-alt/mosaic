## Timeless Question
When does opening the door begin to remove the door that made opening it a choice?

## Venn Domains
- Domain A: Open Welcome (the practiced readiness to make room for a stranger, a need, or an unfinished person before they have earned the room, so that hospitality is not only a reward for the already familiar)
- Domain B: Kept Threshold (the recognition that a door which cannot close has already stopped being a door, and that welcome without a limit can become occupation of the host, the household, or the common work)

**Overlap:** The living hinge where opening and remaining able to close become two conditions of the same welcome — a hospitality that stays generous because the threshold is still intact.

**Color of the Overlap:** Warm Clay  
**Mindset of the Overlap:** Threshold Hospitality — the practiced capacity to open a door without surrendering the right, and the duty, to decide when it closes.
